DRAFT RULES — MANDATORY

1. Emit only fields that have a confirmed Phase 0 source reference or confirmed page evidence.
2. Do not invent any field. A field with no confirmed source is omitted.
3. Do not emit any held field. Held fields remain held regardless of what appears on the page.
4. Do not include any blocked module. Blocked modules are not emitted even if the page contains relevant content.
5. Use schema.org vocabulary only. Do not use non-standard extensions.
6. Use a single JSON-LD script block with @graph for multiple node types.
7. Use https:// URLs only. Do not use http://.
8. Do not include redundant or duplicate properties.
9. Do not include fields that require speculation or inference from non-confirmed sources.
10. Mark the output as NON_PRODUCTION at the top of the draft response.
